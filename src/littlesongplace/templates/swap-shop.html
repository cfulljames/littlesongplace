the swap shop

Welcome to the Swap Shop: a collaborative mode for SaucelessOne's MMiaH.

It works much like &qt;Secret Santa&qt;:

  1. Join the event by entering your PSN name below.
  2. When the event starts, you will be given another name from the list
  3. Start making a song
  4. At the 30 minute mark, release the song as public, and send it to your assigned recipient
  5. You should also receive a song from a different person on the list. It's a surprise!
  6. Spend another 30 minutes remixing the song you received
  7. Release the finished song as public and send it to Sauceless
